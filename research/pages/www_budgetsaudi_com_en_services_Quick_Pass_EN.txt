Quick Pass
We are pleased to announce the implementation of the Quick Pass service, a fast-track airport lane designed to enhance the travel experience for eligible Budget customers.
Quick Pass Service Overview:
· Available at Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam Airports.
· Provided free of charge.
· Exclusively for Corporate and Gold & Platinum customers
· Subject to airport authority regulations and operational availability.
· This service is subject to vehicle and operational availability at the time of request. While we strive to provide seamless support to all eligible customers, fulfillment is not guaranteed for every instance and remains dependent on real-time capacity and seasonal demand.
· Budget does not guarantee processing time and shall not be liable for delays caused by airport authorities or security procedures.
· This service is governed by the laws and regulations of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
